How it works

From application to decision

1. Credit ratios

The raw application becomes the ratios lenders use: loan-to-value, payment-to-income, expenses-to-income and net worth. Skewed amounts are log-scaled.

2. Probability of default

A logistic regression trained on 3,563 loans and tested on 891 unseen ones estimates the chance this loan defaults. It is the same fitted model as in the analysis notebook, running in your browser.

3. Profit-based decision

The model approves the loan when default risk is below 19%. That cut-off maximised expected profit, assuming 60% of a defaulted loan is lost and good loans earn a 15% margin.

This is a portfolio demonstration built on a public research dataset. It is not a real credit product and makes no lending decisions.
